---
title: Workflows
description: Triggers, actions, and conditions on the Graphitti canvas.
---

A workflow is a graph.

- **Trigger**: Manual or webhook. There is no scheduler in this release.
- **Action**: A plugin step such as a Graph query or an ERC-20 transfer.
- **Condition**: Optional branching between actions.

Write actions sign with your linked Privy wallet. Read actions do not need a wallet.
