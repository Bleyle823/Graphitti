title: Privy wallet
description: Embedded wallets linked to your Better Auth user.
---

Graphitti keeps Better Auth for the session. Privy holds the embedded EVM wallet.

After **Connect wallet**, Graphitti verifies the Privy access token and upserts `user_wallets` (`privyUserId`, `privyWalletId`, `address`). Settings shows the address and **gasless enabled** when a wallet is linked.

The Privy plugin can also manage users and wallets through REST if you add `PRIVY_APP_ID` and `PRIVY_APP_SECRET` as a connection.

## Organization wallets

Teams provision a separate **org treasury wallet** when they create a Better Auth organization. Graphitti maps:

- **Owner key quorum** — approves high-value intents
- **Operator policy** — auto payroll under a USDC cap to allowlisted payees
- **Wallet transfer API** — fund treasury and pay contractors without constructing calldata

See [Organization treasury](/treasury/overview).
