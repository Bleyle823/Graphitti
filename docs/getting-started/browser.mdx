---
title: Browser
description: Connect a wallet, open example workflows, and run your first automation.
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect wallet">
    Open [Graphitti](https://graphitti-five.vercel.app) and click **Connect wallet**. Privy authenticates your external wallet and provisions an embedded execution wallet for gasless workflow steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Saved examples">
    On first connect, Circle, Arc, Privy, The Graph, and FPL example workflows are copied into **Saved workflows**. The same examples are also listed on the public **Marketplace** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or edit">
    Add a **Manual** trigger, then an action such as Web3 **Get native balance** or a plugin step from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run">
    Click **Run**. Write actions use the linked embedded wallet and Privy gas sponsorship.
  </Step>
  <Step title="List for agents">
    Open **Marketplace** in the editor toolbar, set a slug and optional Arc USDC price, then publish. Paid calls settle with x402 or Circle nanopayments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Wallet warnings on Vercel

MetaMask and Coinbase may show caution banners on `*.vercel.app` hosts. That is domain verification, not a missing Privy signer. For production, attach a custom domain and register the dapp in Reown Cloud / Coinbase app verification.
